Play Merge Matrix online

Slide numbered tiles across a four-by-four board. When equal-valued tiles touch in the same movement, they combine into one higher number and increase your score.

Play Merge MatrixTry today’s original pick

Controls: Arrow keys, WASD, swipe or the on-screen direction buttons.

How to play Merge Matrix

  1. Start with two tiles on a 4×4 grid.
  2. Slide the board left, right, up or down. Equal tiles that meet combine.
  3. Keep merging while preserving empty spaces. The game challenges you to build towards 2048.

Tips for your next round

  • Try keeping your largest tile near a corner to organise smaller merges around it.
  • Avoid moving in every direction randomly; a consistent edge can make the board easier to manage.
  • Before sliding, check how many open cells you will have afterward so new tiles have room to appear.

What makes this game different?

A fresh small tile appears after a successful move. Planning empty spaces matters as much as chasing the largest number.

Frequently asked question

Do I have to install an app?

No. Merge Matrix is an original HTML canvas browser game and loads from AIO directly.

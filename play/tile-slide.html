Play 3×3 Tile Slide online

A compact sliding-number puzzle with eight numbered tiles and one empty space. The starting board is shuffled using valid tile moves, so every round is reachable from the solved layout.

Controls: Tap or click a tile touching the empty square. On a keyboard use the arrow keys; they move tiles into the gap.

How to play 3×3 Tile Slide

  1. Find the empty square. Only tiles directly beside that gap can move into it.
  2. Tap an adjacent numbered tile, or use the arrow keys to slide a neighbouring tile.
  3. Put numbers 1 through 8 in reading order, with the final bottom-right square empty.

Tips for your next round

  • Start by placing the top row. You can then work on the remaining rows without disturbing it as much.
  • Do not move a tile just because it fits into the gap; think about where the empty space will be next.
  • Watch the Moves counter and try to solve a new shuffled board with fewer moves.

What makes this game different?

There is no timer. It is a puzzle about planning the path of the gap, not racing a countdown.

Frequently asked question

Can every shuffled puzzle be solved?

The original game scrambles a solved board through legal neighbouring moves. That keeps the shuffled arrangement reachable by reversing valid moves.

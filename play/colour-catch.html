Play Colour Catch online

A quick colour-reaction arcade game. Move your basket underneath falling cyan energy while avoiding red hazards and keeping blue energy from falling past you.

Play Colour CatchTry today’s original pick

Controls: Left/right arrow keys, A/D, on-screen buttons, or drag on the board with touch or mouse. Space or P pauses or resumes.

How to play Colour Catch

  1. Start with three lives and watch for the falling coloured items.
  2. Move the basket left or right to collect cyan/blue energy.
  3. Stay away from red drops and try not to miss blue ones. Lose all lives and the round ends.

Tips for your next round

  • Keep the basket near the centre between drops so you can respond in either direction.
  • Look at the next two objects instead of moving toward the nearest one blindly.
  • Avoid chasing a blue drop when doing so would make you collide with a red hazard.

What makes this game different?

Your best score is stored locally in your browser, where available.

Frequently asked question

Is Colour Catch timed?

The goal is to continue scoring while keeping your lives. The game starts with three lives rather than a fixed short countdown.

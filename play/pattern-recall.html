Play 3×3 Pattern Recall online

A memory challenge on nine numbered squares. The game illuminates a sequence of tiles, then asks you to repeat those tiles in the exact same order.

Play 3×3 Pattern RecallTry today’s original pick

Controls: Use mouse, touch or keyboard to activate the numbered 3×3 tile buttons after the sequence is shown.

How to play 3×3 Pattern Recall

  1. Watch the highlighted tile sequence; the tiles are briefly shown one after another.
  2. After the pattern finishes, tap each tile in the same order you saw.
  3. Complete the pattern to advance. Each new level adds another tile to remember.

Tips for your next round

  • Mentally group the squares into top, middle and bottom rows rather than tracking nine separate positions.
  • Say a short pattern like 'top-left, centre, bottom-right' to help recall the order.
  • Avoid switching tabs during a pattern. The game pauses hidden-tab patterns and asks you to restart the round.

What makes this game different?

This is a recognition-and-recall game. Watching carefully matters more than clicking fast.

Frequently asked question

What happens when I pick a wrong tile?

The current run ends, the level reached is shown, and you can begin another round.

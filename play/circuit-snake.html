Play Circuit Snake online

A neon take on a familiar snake game. Guide your growing trail through a rectangular grid and collect golden nodes without crossing the board boundary or your own body.

Play Circuit SnakeTry today’s original pick

Controls: Arrow keys, WASD, on-screen direction buttons or a swipe. Space or P pauses or resumes.

How to play Circuit Snake

  1. Start a round and steer towards the glowing golden node.
  2. Use arrows, WASD or swipe to change direction before you reach a wall.
  3. Every node increases your score and length. The round ends if you hit the edge or your own trail.

Tips for your next round

  • Leave open lanes around your snake so you still have a route after growing longer.
  • Avoid sudden turns into your own body; there is little room for a recovery move.
  • The movement interval shortens as the score grows, so plan turns earlier later in the round.

What makes this game different?

Circuit Snake is a local browser game. It does not need an account or a multiplayer lobby.

Frequently asked question

Does Circuit Snake save my best run?

The original arcade saves your personal best score in browser storage on that device, where storage is available.

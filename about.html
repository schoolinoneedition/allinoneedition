About the website

Games, episode tracking and creative tools in one browser hub.

All in One Edition is a browser-based collection of games and everyday tools. You can try original puzzles and arcade challenges without installing software, browse a larger game library, explore episode guides and use an AI creative workspace. Availability varies by game or service.

Common questions

Do I need to download anything?

The original browser games run in a supported web browser with no separate installation. External publisher-hosted games may have their own requirements.

Does Watch host every anime episode?

No. Watch is primarily an episode guide and locally saved checklist. The site owner can host videos they are authorised to distribute through Backblaze B2 or Vercel Blob. Not every listed episode has a video.

How is watched progress saved?

Watch stores watched ticks in local browser storage on your device and offers a JSON backup export. Clearing that browser's data can remove progress unless you saved a backup.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

The original games and key browsing tools support touch input. Some other games need a keyboard or have separate publisher requirements.

What is Elementor AI?

Elementor is an assistant inside All in One Edition for chatting and permitted creative tasks. It is separate from the game player and may have usage limits or require login for certain functions.

Where to begin

For a quick game, start at the original browser games page, which also links to game-specific guides with controls and tips. For episode lists and watch progress, go to Watch. Use the homepage for the broader library and other tools.

3x3 games to play online

Choose from five 3×3 games on All in One Edition. Two are publisher-hosted maths and escape minigames, and three are original puzzles that use a nine-square board. These games open in your browser without installing an app.

Choose a 3×3 game

Which one should you try?

For times tables and a more active game, try 3x3 Steal an Egg. For keyboard-based challenges, choose 3x3 +1 Keyboard Escape. Prefer a short quiet puzzle? Start with 3×3 Tile Slide or 3×3 Pattern Recall.

If a publisher-hosted game doesn't load, the original 3×3 games above do not depend on the external multiplayer service. You can also browse the original browser game collection for Snake, tile merging and arcade rounds.

3×3 games: common questions

Are these games free and do they need installing?

The games listed here can be opened from a browser without downloading a separate application. Some publisher-hosted games require an internet connection, and availability can vary.

Are all 3×3 games maths games?

No. The two publisher games include times-tables challenges, while Tic Tac Toe is a classic turn-based board game and Pattern Recall is a memory game.

Can I play on my phone or tablet?

The original 3×3 games are designed for touch and keyboard input. Keyboard Escape is primarily keyboard-controlled, so a laptop or computer is the better choice.

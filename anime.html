How to use your anime episode tracker

Search for a show by its title or genre, choose a season, then tick the episodes you've watched. Your checklist is saved on this device, so you can return to it later without creating a separate tracker account.

Can I track episodes and back up my progress?

Yes. Mark episodes as watched or add a series to My List. Use Export watch backup to save a JSON file and Restore watch backup to merge your saved progress on this device.

Where do Watch videos come from?

Watch provides episode information and links to official publisher sources. The verified site owner can also attach authorised self-hosted videos through Backblaze B2. Not every listed episode has a playable video.

How do I find family cartoons or anime?

Use the Anime and Family cartoons filters above, or type a show name, genre or description into Find a show. Select a result to browse its episode guide.

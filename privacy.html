Privacy & cookies

This page explains the main kinds of data involved when using All in One Edition. It is a public description of the website's features, not a substitute for the account owner's full privacy and legal review.

Advertising status: The website contains the AdSense script for site ownership verification. This does not confirm Google approval or that any ad campaign is running. The eligible AdSense account owner must complete review and configure a certified consent message in Google's own dashboard. This website does not use a custom banner pretending to be a certified consent platform.

Progress and preferences on your device

Games can save favourites, recently played titles and progress in your browser. Watch can save checked episodes and a personal series list. Study Hub can save tasks and flashcards. Browser storage is local to the device or browser profile; deleting browser data may remove it. Watch offers an export and restore function for its progress data.

Accounts and user submissions

Some features allow sign-in, community posts, uploaded media and saved conversations. These features use the site's configured services, including Supabase for account and social data. Do not include private details, passwords or other sensitive information in public posts. Removing local browser data is not the same as deleting account or server-stored content.

Media and third-party content

Watch can stream authorised hosted videos from Backblaze B2 through a media service. Other pages may open games and official resources hosted by third parties. Those services may process connection details and have their own privacy practices. Only use external links or uploads that you trust.

Analytics

The site includes Vercel Web Analytics integration code. Collection depends on activation in the hosting dashboard and visitors' browser or privacy settings. Hosting services may also process technical connection and diagnostic data needed to deliver the site. Analytics statistics are not a live count of people watching a page together.

Ads and consent

The AdSense verification script can contact Google when public pages load. Before serving ads, the eligible account owner must complete Google's site review, provide appropriate disclosures and configure a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) with IAB Transparency and Consent Framework support for audiences in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. Ad personalisation and consent choices must follow the applicable requirements. Ad placements should be visibly labelled and kept away from gameplay and interactive controls to minimise accidental clicks.

For information about Google's use of information, see Google advertising technologies. Any live Google-certified privacy message and its consent-choice controls should be configured in the relevant AdSense dashboard, rather than simulated on this page.

Questions or changes

For account-related privacy questions, use the website's official account or support channels. You can clear locally saved browsing data through your browser settings. Server-stored account or community data may need to be handled separately by the site operator. The site operator should review this page as services or their privacy terms change.
